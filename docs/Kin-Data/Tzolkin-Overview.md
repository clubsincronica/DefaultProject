# Tzolkin Overview

The Tzolkin (or Tzolkʼin) is the 260-day sacred calendar used by Maya civilization. It consists of 20 day signs (seals) combined with 13 tones, creating 260 unique kin (day energies).

## Calculating Your Kin

1. Find your birth date in the Gregorian calendar
2. Convert to the Long Count or use a Kin calculator
3. Your Kin = Seal + Tone combination (e.g., Kin 12 = Ik + Cauac)
