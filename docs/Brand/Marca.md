# Marca - Brand Identity Guidelines

## Brand Voice

**Core Identity:** Club Sincrónica is a modern interpretation of ancient Maya wisdom through digital content.

**Tone:**
- **Mystical yet accessible** - Ancient knowledge presented for contemporary audiences
- **Respectful but not preachy** - Honoring traditions without cultural appropriation
- **Energetic but grounded** - Dynamic content with stable foundation
- **Inclusive** - Welcoming all levels of interest and knowledge

**Key Phrases:**
- "Club Sincrónica" (never "Club Sincronica")
- "Kin Maya" / "Tzolkin" (never "King Maya" or "Quim Maya")
- "Oráculo del día" (Oracle of the day)

## Platforms & Content Strategy

### Primary: TikTok & YouTube Shorts
- **Format:** 60-90 second vertical videos
- **Content:** Daily Kin readings with visual oracle
- **Style:** Fast-paced, visually rich, text overlays
- **Music:** Strudel-generated ambient/harmonic soundscapes

### Secondary: Instagram Reels
- **Cross-posting** from TikTok/YouTube
- **Stories:** Behind-the-scenes, polls, engagement

### Long-form: YouTube (10+ minutes)
- **Harmonic meditations** and deep dives
- **Soundscapes** for extended listening
- **Educational content** about Maya calendar

## Visual Style

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep blues, cosmic purples, sacred reds
- **Secondary:** Earth tones (ochre, terracotta, sage)
- **Accent:** Gold, silver, cosmic white
- **Avoid:** Neon colors, harsh contrasts

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, geometric sans-serif
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Maya glyphs:** Custom or respectful reproductions

### Imagery
- **Elements:** Stars, constellations, geometric patterns
- **Nature:** Mountains, water, sacred plants
- **Symbols:** Maya glyphs, astronomical elements
- **Style:** Layered, cosmic, ethereal

### Composition Rules
- **Frame ratio:** 9:16 for shorts, 16:9 for long-form
- **Text placement:** Bottom third for subtitles
- **Branding:** Consistent placement of logo/watermark

## Content Structure

### Daily Kin Video Template
1. **Cartouche (f00):** Frame with kin number and glyph
2. **Oracle:** 3-second delay after cartouche
3. **Reading:** 3-4 frames with interpretation
4. **Outro:** Canonical phrase for the day

### Visual Elements
- **Aspect symbols:** Centered, 160px separation, 1.6 scale
- **Maya numerals:** GAP 26 (no overlap)
- **Subtitles:** 42/36/30 px (never "large")

## Quality Standards

### Do's
- ✅ Use correct spelling: "Club Sincrónica"
- ✅ Respect Maya cultural context
- ✅ Maintain consistent visual style
- ✅ Follow canonical structure

### Don'ts
- ❌ Never use "King Maya" or "Quim Maya"
- ❌ Never exceed 25% frame for kin carta
- ❌ Never use fixed blue (DEEP) for card fills
- ❌ Never rush the oracle appearance
